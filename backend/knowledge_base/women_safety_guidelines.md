# Women Safety Guidelines and Situational Awareness

## 1. Situational Awareness & Solo Transit
Situational awareness is the practice of observing one's surroundings and recognizing potential threats or risks.
* **Scanning the Environment:** Regularly check exit paths, lighting quality, and crowd distributions in transit areas.
* **Distraction Reduction:** Avoid keeping headphones on or using phones in low-visibility or isolated areas.
* **Body Language:** Maintain a confident posture. Walk with a steady pace and purpose.

## 2. Solo Traveling and Commute Planning
When commuting alone, especially late at night:
* **Verify Ride Details:** Always check vehicle number plates and driver IDs before boarding cabs or auto-rickshaws.
* **Route Verification:** Use CausalGuard's Safest Route maps to identify well-lit roads with active street surveillance.
* **Active Sharing:** Share tracking links with your trusted guardians via the Consent-Based Guardian Access feature.

## 3. High-Risk Zone Navigation
If you find yourself in an isolated area:
* **Head for Public Zones:** Move toward commercial stretches, petrol pumps, or round-the-clock pharmacies.
* **Keep Battery Charged:** If your phone battery drops below 15%, CausalGuard activates Low-Battery Mode, notifying guardians of your last coordinates immediately.
