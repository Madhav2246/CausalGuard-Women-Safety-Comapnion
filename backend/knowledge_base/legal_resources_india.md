# Women Legal Resources and Rights in India

## 1. POSH Act (Workplace Safety)
The **Sexual Harassment of Women at Workplace (Prevention, Prohibition and Redressal) Act, 2013** (commonly called the POSH Act) protects women employees in both formal and informal sectors.
* **Internal Complaints Committee (ICC):** Every organization with 10 or more employees is legally required to establish an ICC. Complaints can be submitted in writing directly to this committee.
* **Local Complaints Committee (LCC):** In districts or informal sectors where no ICC exists, complaints are routed to the district-level LCC.

## 2. Indian Penal Code (IPC) / Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita (BNS)
Key criminal sections dealing with harassment and safety:
* **IPC Section 354 (BNS Section 74):** Criminalizes assault or use of criminal force against women with intent to outrage modesty.
* **IPC Section 354A (BNS Section 75):** Defines and criminalizes sexual harassment, including physical contact, demands for sexual favors, or showing pornography.
* **IPC Section 354D (BNS Section 78):** Criminalizes stalking (physical stalking or monitoring digital communications like emails/social media).

## 3. Legal Aid and Support Services
* **National Legal Services Authority (NALSA):** Provides free legal aid and counselors to women across court jurisdictions.
* **One Stop Centers (OSC):** Standardized support centers located across districts providing medical, legal, and counseling services under one roof.
