# Cybercrime Reporting and Digital Safety (India)

## 1. Digital Harassment & Cyber Stalking
Cyber stalking includes repeated monitoring, explicit messaging, doxxing, or sending threats online.
* **Preserve Evidence:** Never delete offensive chats or emails. Take screenshots, copy headers, and save them in the CausalGuard **Evidence Locker**.
* **Do Not Engage:** Avoid engaging with stalkers. Block their accounts immediately after saving evidence.

## 2. National Cyber Crime Reporting Portal
The Government of India hosts a dedicated portal for reporting cybercrimes, particularly those targeting women and children.
* **Official Portal:** [cybercrime.gov.in](https://cybercrime.gov.in)
* **National Helpline:** Dial **1930** (formerly 155260) to report financial cyber fraud and harassment directly.
* **Filing a Complaint:** Complaints can be filed anonymously or using registration profiles. The local cyber cell is legally mandated to investigate.

## 3. Social Media Reporting Mechanisms
Most platforms offer specialized mechanisms for reporting abuse:
* **Report Options:** Use the "Report User" or "Report Post" functions to submit copies of stalker messages directly to safety teams.
* **Locking Profiles:** Enable profile locks and restrict messaging privileges to trusted circles.
