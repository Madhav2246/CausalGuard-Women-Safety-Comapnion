# Campus and Workplace Safety Policies

## 1. Late-Night Campus Walks
When walking across campus or office premises late at night:
* **Prefer Lit Corridors:** Choose pathways with verified active lighting. Avoid dense wooded areas or dark athletic fields.
* **Walking Buddy Mode:** Coordinate walks with trusted classmates or colleagues. You can share your live walk trajectory inside CausalGuard.
* **Security Contacts:** Save the university or office security control room number in your speed dial settings.

## 2. Hostel Check-In Curfew Timers
* **Check-In Alarm:** CausalGuard features a Campus Late-Timer option. If you are returning late to your hostel or home, set a timer.
* **Auto-Notify:** If you fail to check in or dismiss the safety prompt within the countdown limit, CausalGuard triggers an alert to hostel wardens and guardians automatically.

## 3. Unsafe Zone Feedback & Auditing
* **Auditing Unsafe Zones:** If you encounter broken streetlights, aggressive dogs, or loitering hotspots, drop an unsafe zone marker.
* **Aggregated Signals:** Your feedback helps compile the local campus safety heatmap, adjusting risk scores dynamically for other students.
