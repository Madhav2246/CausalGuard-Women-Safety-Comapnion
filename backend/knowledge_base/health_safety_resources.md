# Health Safety and Supportive Commute Guidance

## 1. Safety Support Disclaimer
* **Disclaimer:** CausalGuard does not provide medical diagnoses or medical advice. The Health Safety Mode is a supportive commute navigation option designed to minimize physical transit strain and maximize proximity to pharmacies and healthcare facilities.

## 2. Period Discomfort Mode Routing
When experiencing period pain or discomfort during commutes:
* **Pharmacy Proximity:** CausalGuard re-weights routing options to prefer main streets with active pharmacies, medical clinics, and resting spots.
* **Low-Strenuous Paths:** Reduces walking distance recommendations and avoids unpaved or steep roads.
* **Support Reminders:** Log reminders for hydration or medicines through the Health Support panel.

## 3. Pregnancy Safety Commutes
For solo commutes during pregnancy:
* **Responder Access:** Prioritizes routes that stay within 500m of active healthcare providers or hospitals.
* **Vibration/Strain Warning:** Flags bumpy roads or routes with poor street quality to minimize physical jarring.
* **Easy Access Escapes:** Recommends main highways with immediate taxi/cab access points.
