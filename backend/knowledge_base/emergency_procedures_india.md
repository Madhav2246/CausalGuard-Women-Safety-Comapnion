# Emergency Procedures and Distress Handling Protocols (India)

## 1. Immediate Action in Case of Threat
If you feel you are in immediate danger:
* **Activate SOS:** Press the primary red SOS button on your CausalGuard dashboard, or call out using your Safe Word if voice monitoring is enabled.
* **Make Noise:** Attract public attention. Use a whistle, or scream to alert passersby.
* **Seek Refuge:** Move immediately into a crowded space, shop, or a nearby apartment building security desk.

## 2. Contacting Emergency Services
In India, the unified emergency number is **112**.
* **National Emergency Number (112):** Integrates police (100), fire (101), and ambulance (102) response desks.
* **Call Button:** Use the quick-dial link on the CausalGuard screen (`tel:112`).
* **Relay Details:** Clearly state your location (landmarks, cross-streets) and the nature of the threat to the dispatcher.

## 3. Police Response & Support
* **Zero FIR:** You can file a FIR (First Information Report) at *any* police station, regardless of where the incident occurred. The police are legally bound to register it and transfer it to the appropriate station.
* **Simulated Dispatch MVP:** In CausalGuard, the Police/Admin Console receives active alerts directly for hackathon testing purposes.
