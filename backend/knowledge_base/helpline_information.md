# Official Safety and Emergency Helplines (India)

Below is a list of verified emergency response numbers that can be dialed across India. These numbers are integrated as quick-links (`tel:`) inside CausalGuard:

* **112 - National Emergency Response System (ERS)**
  * Single emergency number for immediate police, fire, or medical dispatch. Replaces the older 100, 101, 102 lines.
* **181 - Women's Helpline (NCW)**
  * A 24x7 helpline operated by the National Commission for Women (NCW) providing crisis support, counseling, and legal assistance referrals.
* **14490 - NCW Safety Support Desk**
  * Secondary NCW support desk providing legal advice and safety coordination resources.
* **1930 - Cybercrime National Helpline**
  * Direct reporting helpline for online harassment, identity theft, cyber stalking, and digital financial fraud.
* **100 - Police Helpline (Secondary/Legacy)**
  * Connects to local police dispatchers in areas where ERS 112 is still routing traffic.
* **1098 - Childline India**
  * Specialized 24-hour helpline support for children in distress or facing abuse.
