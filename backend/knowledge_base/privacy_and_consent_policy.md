# Privacy and Consent Management Policy

## 1. Local-First Design Philosophy
CausalGuard prioritizes user privacy. Sensitive information is kept local whenever possible.
* **No Raw ID Storage:** Government ID documents used during the verification check are processed locally on the client or deleted immediately after checking. Only the "Verified" status flag is written to the database.
* **Federated Feedback Aggregation:** Journey safety ratings are anonymized and stripped of personal route starting/destination coordinates before compiling community-level safety index weights.

## 2. Granular Consent Controls
You retain full control over how your location and safety data is shared:
* **SOS-Only Tracking:** Guardians cannot query your live coordinates unless an active SOS or warning timer has been triggered.
* **Journey-Only Tracking:** Permissions allow guardians to track location only during an active journey. Access is revoked automatically once you press "End Journey."
* **Revocability:** Consents can be toggled off at any moment through the Privacy Control Panel.

## 3. History Purge & Right to Erasure
* **History Erasure:** Users can permanently delete all commute logs, location updates, and evidence locker attachments instantly.
* **Compliance:** Aligns with standard privacy regulations (like the Indian DPDP Act) supporting user right to erasure.
