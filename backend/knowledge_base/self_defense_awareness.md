# Self-Defense Awareness and Safety Tools

## 1. Verbal & Psychological Deterrence
De-escalation and verbal defense can discourage potential attackers before physical conflict occurs:
* **Strong Voice:** Use a loud, assertive tone. Shout commands like "BACK OFF!" or "STOP!" to assert boundaries.
* **Eye Contact:** Maintain direct, firm eye contact. This signals confidence and situational awareness.
* **Creating Distance:** Keep at least two arm lengths of space between you and a suspicious individual.

## 2. Basic Physical Self-Defense Techniques
If physical contact is unavoidable, target vulnerable areas to disable the attacker and escape:
* **Target Areas:** Eyes (strike or gouge), Nose (palm strike upward), Throat (strike or press), and Groin (kick or knee strike).
* **Escape Focus:** The goal of self-defense is not to win a fight, but to disable the attacker long enough to run to a public, well-lit safety zone.

## 3. Allowed Self-Defense Tools
In India, certain personal protection tools are legally permitted:
* **Pepper Spray:** Highly effective at a distance. Store it in an easily accessible pocket, not at the bottom of a bag.
* **Personal Alarms:** Devices that emit high-decibel sirens to disorient attackers and attract public attention.
* **CausalGuard Integration:** Silent triggers like the Safe Word or SOS panel notify responders without sounding loud alarms if you choose.
